## Question
Walk me through a project you're proud of, end to end.

## Structure
Cover: the problem/context, your specific role and decisions (not just "the team did X"), a technical challenge you hit and how you solved it, and the measurable outcome or impact.

## My Story
Fill in a real example: pick something where you can go deep on at least one hard technical decision if the interviewer probes it -- vague, high-level answers are the most common way this question goes badly. Have a number ready for impact (latency reduced, users affected, cost saved) even if it's an estimate, and be ready to explain a trade-off you made, not just the parts that went smoothly.
