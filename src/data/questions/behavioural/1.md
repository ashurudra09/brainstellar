## Question
Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate on a technical decision. How did you handle it?

## Structure
Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Interviewers are listening for *how* you resolved the disagreement, not for who turned out to be right.

## My Story
Fill in a real example before your interview: what was the disagreement actually about (be specific -- "we disagreed on the caching strategy for the checkout service," not "we disagreed on architecture"), what did you do to move it forward (propose a small test, bring in data, ask a third opinion, timebox the discussion), and what was the actual outcome -- including if your position didn't win, and what you learned from that.
