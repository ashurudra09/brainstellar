## Question
Python list append() vs extend() behaviour.

## Answer
append adds its argument as one element; extend adds each element of an iterable

## Solution
\[1\].append(\[2,3\]) gives \[1,\[2,3\]\]; \[1\].extend(\[2,3\]) gives \[1,2,3\].
