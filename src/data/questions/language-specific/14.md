## Question
Output?

```c
char s[] = "hello";
printf("%zu %zu", sizeof(s), strlen(s));
```

## Options
- 6 5
- 5 5
- 8 5
- 6 6

## Answer
**A.** 6 5

## Solution
sizeof counts the terminating '\\0' (6). strlen stops before it (5).
