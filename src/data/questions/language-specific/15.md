## Question
Output?

```c
#define SQ(x) x*x
printf("%d", SQ(2+3));
```

## Options
- 11
- 25
- 13
- 10

## Answer
**A.** 11

## Solution
Text substitution gives 2+3\*2+3 = 2+6+3 = 11. Writing #define SQ(x) ((x)\*(x)) would give 25.
