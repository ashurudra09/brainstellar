## Question
Output?

```java
System.out.println("MathWorks".substring(4, 6) + " " + "MathWorks".indexOf('W') + " " + "abc".compareTo("abd"));
```

## Options
- Wo 4 -1
- Wor 4 -1
- Wo 5 1
- Wo 4 1

## Answer
**A.** Wo 4 -1

## Solution
substring(begin, end) excludes end, so it takes indices 4 and 5. compareTo returns 'c' − 'd' = −1.
