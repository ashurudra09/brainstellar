## Question
Output?

```c
int a[] = {10, 20, 30, 40};
int *p = a;
printf("%d %d", *(p + 2), *p + 2);
```

## Options
- 30 12
- 30 30
- 12 30
- 20 12

## Answer
**A.** 30 12

## Solution
\*(p+2) is a\[2\] = 30. \*p + 2 dereferences first, then adds: 10 + 2 = 12.
