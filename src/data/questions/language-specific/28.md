## Question
Output?

```c
printf("%d", 5 & 3 | 4 ^ 1);
```

## Options
- 5
- 1
- 4
- 7

## Answer
**A.** 5

## Solution
Precedence is & then ^ then \|. 5&3 = 1, 4^1 = 5, and 1\|5 = 5.
