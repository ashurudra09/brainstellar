## Question
Output?

```cpp
vector<int> v = {1, 2, 3};
v.reserve(10);
cout << v.size() << " " << v.capacity();
```

## Options
- 3 10
- 10 10
- 3 3
- 10 3

## Answer
**A.** 3 10

## Solution
reserve only grows the capacity. Use resize to change the size.
