## Question
Which of these is a checked exception?

## Options
- IOException
- NullPointerException
- ArithmeticException
- ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException

## Answer
**A.** IOException

## Solution
Checked exceptions extend Exception but not RuntimeException, and must be caught or declared with throws.
