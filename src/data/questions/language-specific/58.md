## Question
Which operator can NOT be overloaded in C++?

## Options
- ?: (ternary)
- \[\]
- ()
- \<\<

## Answer
**A.** ?: (ternary)

## Solution
The non-overloadable operators are :: . .\* ?: and sizeof (also typeid and the casts).
