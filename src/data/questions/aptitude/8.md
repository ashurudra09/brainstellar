## Question
8 identical-looking balls, one is heavier. Minimum weighings on a balance to find it for sure?

## Options
- 2
- 3
- 4
- 7

## Answer
**A.** 2

## Solution
Split 3-3-2. Weigh 3 vs 3: the heavy ball is in the heavier group of 3, or in the 2 left out if they balance. One more weighing finds it. In general it takes ⌈log₃ n⌉ weighings.
