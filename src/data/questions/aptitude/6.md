## Question
Chocolates packed equally leave 10 over; with 2 more per packet there's a shortfall of 4. How many packets?

## Options
- 3
- 5
- 7
- 9

## Answer
**C.** 7

## Solution
kn + 10 = (k + 2)n − 4, so 2n = 14.
