## Question
Grouped frequency of last-name lengths: 1-4:5, 4-7:10, 7-10:50, 10-13:25, 13-16:5, 16-19:5. Find the mean length.

## Options
- 10
- \6.25
- 8
- \9.45

## Answer
**D.** \9.45

## Solution
Use the class midpoints 2.5, 5.5, …, 17.5: Σfx = 940 and Σf = 100.
