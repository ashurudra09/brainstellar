## Question
Two fair dice are rolled. P(sum ≥ 10)?

## Options
- 1/6
- 1/12
- 5/36
- 1/4

## Answer
**A.** 1/6

## Solution
Sum 10: 3 ways, sum 11: 2 ways, sum 12: 1 way. That's 6/36 = 1/6.
