## Question
A boat goes 20 km/h downstream and 12 km/h upstream. Speed of the stream?

## Options
- 4 km/h
- 8 km/h
- 16 km/h
- 6 km/h

## Answer
**A.** 4 km/h

## Solution
Stream = (down − up)/2 = 4. Boat = (down + up)/2 = 16.
