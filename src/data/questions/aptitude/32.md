## Question
Trains of 120 m and 80 m run in opposite directions at 50 km/h and 40 km/h. Time to cross each other?

## Options
- 8 s
- 10 s
- 20 s
- \7.2 s

## Answer
**A.** 8 s

## Solution
Moving in opposite directions, the relative speed is 90 km/h = 25 m/s. The distance is the sum of the lengths, 200 m. 200/25 = 8 s.
