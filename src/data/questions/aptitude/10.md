## Question
Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Q1 120, Q2 150, Q3 135, Q4 180. Which quarter had the highest quarter-on-quarter growth %?

## Options
- Q2
- Q3
- Q4
- Q1

## Answer
**C.** Q4

## Solution
Q2: +25%, Q3: −10%, Q4: 45/135 = +33.3%.
