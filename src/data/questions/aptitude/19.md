## Question
40 play football, 35 cricket, 30 hockey. 15 play F&C, 12 C&H, 10 F&H, and 5 play all three. How many play at least one?

## Options
- 73
- 68
- 78
- 83

## Answer
**A.** 73

## Solution
Inclusion-exclusion: 105 − 37 + 5 = 73.
