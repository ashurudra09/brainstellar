## Question
Highest power of 24 that divides 150!?

## Options
- 42
- 44
- 46
- 48

## Answer
**D.** 48

## Solution
24 = 2³ × 3. Factors of 2 in 150! (Legendre's formula): 75+37+18+9+4+2+1 = 146, which makes ⌊146/3⌋ = 48 copies of 2³. Factors of 3: 50+16+5+1 = 72. The scarcer one limits the answer: 48.
