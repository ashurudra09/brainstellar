## Question
A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5. Find A:B:C.

## Options
- 8:12:15
- 2:3:5
- 2:12:5
- 4:6:5

## Answer
**A.** 8:12:15

## Solution
Scale both ratios so B is 12: A:B = 8:12 and B:C = 12:15.
