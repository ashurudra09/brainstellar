## Question
An event has probability 0.6 on each independent trial. What is the probability it happens 5 times in a row?

## Options
- ≈ 0.078
- \0.3
- \0.6
- ≈ 0.13

## Answer
**A.** ≈ 0.078

## Solution
Independent events multiply: 0.6⁵ = 0.07776. (This question type was reported in the 2025 math section.)
