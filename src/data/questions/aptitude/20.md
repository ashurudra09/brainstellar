## Question
Of 120 people, 70 like A, 60 like B and 50 like C. 30 like A&B, 25 B&C, 20 A&C, and 10 like all three. How many like exactly one?

## Options
- 60
- 55
- 65
- 50

## Answer
**A.** 60

## Solution
Exactly one = Σsingles − 2·Σpairs + 3·triple = 180 − 150 + 30 = 60. Check: exactly two = 75 − 30 = 45, all three = 10, and 60 + 45 + 10 = 115 = the union.
