## Question
A price rises by 20% and then falls by 20%. Net change?

## Options
- 4% loss
- No change
- 4% gain
- 2% loss

## Answer
**A.** 4% loss

## Solution
\1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96. Going +a% then −a% always loses a²/100 %.
