## Question
What is the smallest number that must be added to 1056 to make it divisible by 23?

## Options
- 2
- 21
- 3
- 1

## Answer
**A.** 2

## Solution
1056 = 23·45 + 21. Adding 23 − 21 = 2 gives 1058 = 23·46.
