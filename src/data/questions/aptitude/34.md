## Question
A car goes A→B at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h. Average speed?

## Options
- 48 km/h
- 50 km/h
- 45 km/h
- 52 km/h

## Answer
**A.** 48 km/h

## Solution
For equal distances use the harmonic mean: 2·60·40/(60+40) = 48. Averaging the speeds (50) is the trap.
