## Question
Sales were 200 in 2021, 250 in 2022 and 300 in 2023. Which statements are true? I: growth 2021→22 was 25%. II: growth 2022→23 was 20%. III: both years grew by the same percentage.

## Options
- I and II only
- I only
- All three
- II and III only

## Answer
**A.** I and II only

## Solution
50/200 = 25% and 50/250 = 20%. The absolute increase is the same, but the percentage isn't. (Likely question style: a table plus 'which conclusions are correct' was reported.)
