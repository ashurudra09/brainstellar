## Question
Two cards are drawn from a deck without replacement. P(both aces)?

## Options
- 1/221
- 1/169
- 1/26
- 4/221

## Answer
**A.** 1/221

## Solution
(4/52)·(3/51) = 12/2652 = 1/221. With replacement it would be 1/169.
