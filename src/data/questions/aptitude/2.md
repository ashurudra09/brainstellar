## Question
Simplify (x+y)^2 − (x−y)^2.

## Options
- 4xy
- 4x^2
- 4y^2
- 2x^2+2y^2

## Answer
**A.** 4xy

## Solution
Expand both squares; the x² and y² terms cancel, leaving 2xy − (−2xy).
