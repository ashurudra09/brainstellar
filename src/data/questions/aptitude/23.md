## Question
A 3-person committee is chosen from 5 men and 4 women, with at least one woman. How many ways?

## Options
- 74
- 84
- 80
- 60

## Answer
**A.** 74

## Solution
Total minus the all-male committees: C(9,3) − C(5,3) = 84 − 10 = 74.
