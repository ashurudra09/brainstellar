## Question
6 people sit around a round table. In how many ways can two particular people sit together?

## Options
- 48
- 120
- 240
- 24

## Answer
**A.** 48

## Solution
Glue the pair into one unit: 5 units in a circle can be arranged (5−1)! = 24 ways, times 2 for the pair's order = 48.
