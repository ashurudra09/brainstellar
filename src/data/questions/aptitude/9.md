## Question
Five people A–E sit in a row. B is right of A (adjacent), C is at an end, D sits next to both B and E, and A is not at an end. Who sits in the middle?

## Options
- B
- D
- A
- E

## Answer
**A.** B

## Solution
Fix the constrained block A B D E (B right after A, D between B and E). A can't be at an end, so C takes the left end: C A B D E, the only arrangement that works. B is 3rd.
