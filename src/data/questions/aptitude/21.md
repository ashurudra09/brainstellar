## Question
P finished after T but before Q. R finished after Q. S finished immediately after T. Who finished 3rd?

## Options
- P
- Q
- S
- R

## Answer
**A.** P

## Solution
T S is fixed as a pair. T comes before P, which comes before Q and then R, so the order is T S P Q R. 3rd is P. (Likely question style: the 2025 paper had a relative-ordering question.)
