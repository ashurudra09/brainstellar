## Question
All cats are animals. Some animals are dogs. Does 'Some cats are dogs' follow?

## Options
- Yes
- No
- Only if all dogs are animals
- Cannot say

## Answer
**B.** No

## Solution
The dogs can lie in the part of 'animals' outside 'cats'. A conclusion must hold in every diagram.
