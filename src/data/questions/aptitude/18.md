## Question
Of 100 students, 60 like tea, 50 like coffee and 30 like both. How many like neither?

## Options
- 10
- 20
- 30
- 40

## Answer
**B.** 20

## Solution
\|T∪C\| = 60 + 50 − 30 = 80, so 100 − 80 = 20 like neither.
