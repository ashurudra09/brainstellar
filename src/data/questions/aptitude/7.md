## Question
Which is larger: 100^99 or 99^100? (listed as sample question; suggested combinatorial approach)

## Options
- 100^99
- 99^100

## Answer
**B.** 99^100

## Solution
Compare 100·ln 99 with 99·ln 100. Equivalently, n^(1/n) decreases for n ≥ 3, so 99^(1/99) \> 100^(1/100); raise both sides to the power 9900.
