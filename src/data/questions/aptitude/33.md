## Question
A 150 m train moving at 54 km/h crosses a 250 m platform. How long does it take?

## Options
- ≈ 26.7 s
- ≈ 16.7 s
- 10 s
- 30 s

## Answer
**A.** ≈ 26.7 s

## Solution
54 km/h = 15 m/s. The train has to cover its own length plus the platform: 400/15 ≈ 26.7 s. (A pole would take 150/15 = 10 s.)
