## Question
P(A) = 0.5 and P(B) = 0.4, and A and B are independent. P(exactly one of them occurs)?

## Options
- \0.5
- \0.7
- \0.2
- \0.9

## Answer
**A.** \0.5

## Solution
A and not B: 0.5·0.6 = 0.3. B and not A: 0.4·0.5 = 0.2. Total 0.5.
