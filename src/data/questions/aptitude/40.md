## Question
A finishes a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. Working together?

## Options
- 6 days
- \12.5 days
- 5 days
- 25 days

## Answer
**A.** 6 days

## Solution
Per day they do 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6 of the job, so together they take 6 days.
