## Question
Machine A makes 60% of items (2% defective) and machine B makes 40% (3% defective). An item is defective. P(it came from A)?

## Options
- \0.4
- \0.5
- \0.6
- \0.012

## Answer
**B.** \0.5

## Solution
Bayes: 0.6·0.02 / (0.6·0.02 + 0.4·0.03) = 0.012 / 0.024 = 0.5.
