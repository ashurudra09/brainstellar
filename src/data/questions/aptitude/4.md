## Question
Of 100 students, 60 play cricket, 40 football, 20 both. How many play neither?

## Options
- 10
- 20
- 30
- 40

## Answer
**B.** 20

## Solution
At least one sport = 60 + 40 − 20 = 80, so neither = 100 − 80.
