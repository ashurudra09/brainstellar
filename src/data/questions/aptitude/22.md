## Question
How many arrangements of the letters of MATHWORKS keep the vowels together?

## Options
- 80640
- 40320
- 362880
- 10080

## Answer
**A.** 80640

## Solution
The 9 letters are all distinct, and the vowels are A and O. Glue them into one block: that leaves 8 units, giving 8! = 40320, times 2! for the order inside the block = 80640.
