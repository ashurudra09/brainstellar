## Question
Goods are marked 25% above cost price and sold at a 10% discount. Profit %?

## Options
- \12.5%
- 15%
- 10%
- 35%

## Answer
**A.** \12.5%

## Solution
\1.25 × 0.9 = 1.125, so the profit is 12.5%.
