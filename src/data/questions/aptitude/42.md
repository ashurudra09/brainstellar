## Question
2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

## Options
- 42
- 40
- 36
- 44

## Answer
**A.** 42

## Solution
The differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, so the next difference is 12 → 42. Equivalently, the nth term is n(n+1).
