## Question
An 18 cm cube has 2 opposite faces red and 4 blue. It is cut into 729 equal cubes. How many small cubes have at least one red AND one blue face?

## Options
- 56
- 64
- 72
- 48

## Answer
**B.** 64

## Solution
729 = 9³, so 9 cubes per edge. A cube touches both colours exactly when it lies on the border ring of a red face: 4 × 9 − 4 = 32 per ring (corners counted once), and two red faces give 64.
