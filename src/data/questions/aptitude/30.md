## Question
Which digit x makes 5x2738 divisible by 11?

## Options
- 6
- 4
- 2
- 8

## Answer
**A.** 6

## Solution
Alternating sum from the right: 8 − 3 + 7 − 2 + x − 5 = 5 + x, which must be ≡ 0 (mod 11), so x = 6. Check: 562738 = 11 × 51158.
