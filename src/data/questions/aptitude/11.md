## Question
In a pie chart of a ₹5,00,000 budget, the marketing slice is 72°. Marketing spend?

## Options
- ₹1,00,000
- ₹72,000
- ₹1,20,000
- ₹80,000

## Answer
**A.** ₹1,00,000

## Solution
72/360 = 20%.
