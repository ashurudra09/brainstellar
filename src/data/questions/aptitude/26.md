## Question
A fair coin is tossed 4 times. P(exactly 2 heads)?

## Options
- 3/8
- 1/2
- 1/4
- 5/16

## Answer
**A.** 3/8

## Solution
C(4,2)/2⁴ = 6/16 = 3/8.
