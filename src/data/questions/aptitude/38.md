## Question
Difference between CI and SI on ₹10,000 at 10% per year for 2 years?

## Options
- ₹100
- ₹200
- ₹110
- ₹0

## Answer
**A.** ₹100

## Solution
For 2 years, CI − SI = P(r/100)² = 10000 × 0.01 = 100.
