## Question
When to apply log, square-root or Box-Cox transformations (listed alongside CSV MCQ round; may be interview).

## Answer
Log for right-skewed positive data; square root for counts; Box-Cox (positive data) searches for the best power λ

## Solution
Each one reduces skew and stabilises variance.
