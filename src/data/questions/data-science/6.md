## Question
Methods to identify outliers in a CSV dataset (listed by author alongside the 11-MCQ CSV round; may be interview).

## Answer
IQR rule (outside Q1 − 1.5·IQR or Q3 + 1.5·IQR), z-score \> 3, box plots, Isolation Forest / LOF
