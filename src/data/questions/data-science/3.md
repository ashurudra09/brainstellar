## Question
Homoscedasticity vs heteroscedasticity of residuals.

## Answer
Homoscedastic: residual variance is constant across x. Heteroscedastic: it changes (often a funnel shape).

## Solution
Heteroscedasticity breaks OLS standard errors; fix with robust SEs, WLS or a log transform.
