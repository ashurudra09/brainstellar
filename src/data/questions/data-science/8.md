## Question
df has index \[10, 20, 30\] and columns a = \[1, 2, 3\], b = \[4, 5, 6\]. What are df.loc\[20, 'a'\] and df.iloc\[0, 1\]?

## Options
- 2 and 4
- 1 and 4
- 2 and 5
- Error and 4

## Answer
**A.** 2 and 4

## Solution
loc selects by label (the row labelled 20, so a = 2). iloc selects by position (row 0, column 1, so b = 4).
