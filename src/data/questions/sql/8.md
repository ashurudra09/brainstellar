## Question
A table has 10 rows; column x is NULL in 3 of them. What do COUNT(\*) and COUNT(x) return?

## Options
- 10 and 10
- 10 and 7
- 7 and 7
- 7 and 10

## Answer
**B.** 10 and 7

## Solution
COUNT(\*) counts rows no matter what they contain. COUNT(x) counts only non-NULL values of x.
