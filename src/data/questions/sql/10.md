## Question
Salaries 100, 90, 90, 80. DENSE\_RANK() over salary DESC gives 80 the rank:

## Options
- 2
- 3
- 4
- 5

## Answer
**B.** 3

## Solution
DENSE\_RANK gives ties the same rank with no gaps: 1, 2, 2, 3. RANK would skip a number after the tie and give 4.
