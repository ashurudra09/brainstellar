## Question
Table A has 5 rows, table B has 4 rows. Rows returned by A CROSS JOIN B?

## Options
- 9
- 5
- 20
- 4

## Answer
**C.** 20

## Solution
A cross join pairs every row of A with every row of B: 5 × 4 = 20.
