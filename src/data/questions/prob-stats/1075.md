## Question
An archer hits with p = 0.3 and shoots 10 times. Mean and variance of hits?

## Options
- 3, 2.1
- 3, 2.4
- 7, 2.1
- 7, 2.4

## Answer
**A.** 3, 2.1

## Solution
Binomial with n = 10, p = 0.3: mean = np = 3, variance = np(1−p) = 3 × 0.7 = 2.1. (This exact question was in the PA paper.)
