## Question
Simple linear regression of exam score on study hours has slope 5 and intercept 10. Which is the regression line?

## Options
- y = 5x + 10
- y = 10x + 5
- y = 105 + b
- y = 5 − 10b

## Answer
**A.** y = 5x + 10

## Solution
y = intercept + slope · x.
