## Question
Claimed mean weight 500 g; sample of 36 has mean 492 g and SD 18 g. Compute the Z-score.

## Options
- −2.67
- \1.33
- \2.67
- −1.33

## Answer
**A.** −2.67

## Solution
z = (492 − 500) / (18/√36) = −8/3.
