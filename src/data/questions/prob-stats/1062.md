## Question
Noisy cubic data fit by a linear model M1 and a degree-5 polynomial M5. Compare their bias and variance.

## Options
- B1≤B5, V1≤V5
- B1≥B5, V1≤V5
- B1≤B5, V1≥V5
- B1≥B5, V1≥V5

## Answer
**B.** B1≥B5, V1≤V5

## Solution
The linear model can't fit a cubic (high bias) but is stable (low variance); the degree-5 polynomial is the reverse.
