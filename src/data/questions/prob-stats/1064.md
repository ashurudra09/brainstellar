## Question
A coin is either fair or two-headed (equally likely). It shows two heads. Probability it is fair?

## Options
- 1/3
- 4/13
- 1/5
- 2/5

## Answer
**C.** 1/5

## Solution
(1/2 · 1/4) / (1/2 · 1/4 + 1/2 · 1) = (1/8) / (5/8).
