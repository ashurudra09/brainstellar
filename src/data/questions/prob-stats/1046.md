## Question
Three fair dice are rolled. Probability the sum is exactly 10?

## Options
- 6/216
- 10/216
- 12/216
- 9/216

## Answer
27/216 (= 1/8) — not among the reported options

## Solution
There are 27 ordered triples summing to 10. The reported options were probably misremembered.
