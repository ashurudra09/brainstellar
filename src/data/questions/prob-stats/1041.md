## Question
Roll a die repeatedly, adding faces until the running total reaches 100 or more. Which face, 1 or 6, appears more often in such sequences?

## Options
- 1
- 6

## Answer
They appear equally often on average

## Solution
Each roll is decided before its result is seen, so each roll is uniform and E\[#1s\] = E\[#6s\] = E\[#rolls\]/6 ≈ 4.84.
