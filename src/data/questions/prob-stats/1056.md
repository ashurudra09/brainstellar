## Question
Two distinct squares are picked at random on a chessboard. Probability they share a side?

## Options
- 8/13
- 17/18
- 5/13
- 1/18

## Answer
**D.** 1/18

## Solution
Adjacent pairs = 2·8·7 = 112, and C(64,2) = 2016.
