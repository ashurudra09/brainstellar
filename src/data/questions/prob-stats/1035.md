## Question
What do alpha and beta denote in hypothesis testing (Type I vs Type II error)?

## Answer
α = P(Type I) = P(reject H₀ \| H₀ true); β = P(Type II) = P(fail to reject \| H₀ false)

## Solution
Power = 1 − β.
