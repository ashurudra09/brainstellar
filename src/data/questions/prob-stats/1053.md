## Question
James solves a problem with probability 3/4, Simon with 4/5, independently. Probability neither solves it?

## Options
- 1/20
- 3/20
- 13/20
- 2/15

## Answer
**A.** 1/20

## Solution
P(neither) = (1 − 3/4)(1 − 4/5) = 1/4 · 1/5.
