## Question
Box with 3 red, 2 blue; draw twice without replacement. P(second red \| first red)?

## Options
- 3/5
- 2/5
- 1/2
- 3/4

## Answer
**C.** 1/2

## Solution
After one red is drawn, 2 red remain among 4 balls.
