## Question
Weights of 10 students: 45, 50, 52, 55, 60, 62, 65, 68, 70, 75 kg. Mean weight?

## Options
- \58.5
- \60.2
- \62.6
- \64.6

## Answer
**B.** \60.2

## Solution
The weights sum to 602, and 602/10 = 60.2.
