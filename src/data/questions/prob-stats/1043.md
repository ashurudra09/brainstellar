## Question
The posterior distribution is proportional to which expression?

## Options
- Prior / Likelihood
- Likelihood / Prior
- Likelihood × Prior
- Prior + Likelihood

## Answer
**C.** Likelihood × Prior

## Solution
Bayes: posterior = likelihood × prior / evidence, and the evidence doesn't depend on the parameter.
