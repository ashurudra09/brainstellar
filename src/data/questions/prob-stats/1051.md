## Question
Bag has 8 red and 6 black balls; two drawn without replacement. P(second red \| first black)?

## Options
- 8/13
- (garbled in source)
- 7/13
- 11/13

## Answer
**A.** 8/13

## Solution
After a black ball is removed, 8 red remain out of 13.
