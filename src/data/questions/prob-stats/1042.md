## Question
A 3-person committee is chosen at random from 5 men and 4 women. Probability exactly 2 are women?

## Options
- 10/84
- 40/84
- 30/84
- 20/84

## Answer
**C.** 30/84

## Solution
C(4,2)·C(5,1) / C(9,3) = 6·5 / 84.
