## Question
X \~ N(50, 5²). P(X \> 55)?

## Options
- \2.28%
- \15.87%
- 50%
- \97.72%

## Answer
**B.** \15.87%

## Solution
z = (55 − 50)/5 = 1, so 55 is one standard deviation above the mean. The area beyond z = 1 is 15.87%. 2.28% would be z = 2.
