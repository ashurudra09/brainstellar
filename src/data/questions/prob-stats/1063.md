## Question
Variance of the data {2, 4, 6, 8, 10}?

## Options
- 4
- 6
- 8
- 10

## Answer
**C.** 8

## Solution
Mean 6; squared deviations 16 + 4 + 0 + 4 + 16 = 40, and 40/5 = 8 (population variance). The sample variance (÷4) would be 10.
