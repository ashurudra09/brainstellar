## Question
Four points chosen uniformly at random on a sphere's surface; probability the sphere's centre lies inside their tetrahedron?

## Answer
1/8

## Solution
Wendel's theorem: P = 2⁻ⁿ⁺¹ Σₖ₌₀^(d−1) C(n−1, k), with n = 4 and d = 3. Or: of the 16 sign choices for the 4 points, 2 contain the centre.
