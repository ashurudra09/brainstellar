## Question
Expected number of fair-coin flips until two heads in a row (HH)?

## Options
- 6
- 4
- 3
- 8

## Answer
**A.** 6

## Solution
Let E be the expected flips from scratch and E₁ the expected flips after one H. E = ½(1 + E₁) + ½(1 + E) and E₁ = ½·1 + ½(1 + E), which give E = 6. HT takes only 4.
