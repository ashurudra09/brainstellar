## Question
Two statements about unbiased estimators of μ and σ^2 (sample SD; (n−1)/n-scaled variance). Which are true?

## Options
- 1 only
- 2 only
- Both
- Neither

## Answer
**D.** Neither

## Solution
The sample SD is a biased estimator of σ (Jensen), and dividing by n instead of n − 1 biases the variance. The exact statements weren't recalled in the source.
