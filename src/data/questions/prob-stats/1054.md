## Question
Three people toss one coin each simultaneously. Probability all show the same face?

## Options
- 1/4
- 1/6
- 1/8
- 1/12

## Answer
**A.** 1/4

## Solution
2 favourable outcomes (HHH, TTT) out of 8.
