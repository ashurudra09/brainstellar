## Question
One coin is fair, the other lands heads with probability 3/4. A random coin shows two heads. Probability it is the fair coin?

## Options
- 13/32
- 4/13
- 2/13
- 19/32

## Answer
**B.** 4/13

## Solution
P(HH \| fair) = 1/4, P(HH \| biased) = 9/16. Posterior = (1/4) / (1/4 + 9/16).
