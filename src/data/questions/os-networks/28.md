## Question
With FIFO page replacement and 3 frames, how many page faults for 1,2,3,4,1,2,5,1,2,3,4,5?

## Options
- 9
- 10
- 8
- 12

## Answer
**A.** 9

## Solution
9 faults. With 4 frames it rises to 10: Belady's anomaly, which only FIFO-like algorithms show.
