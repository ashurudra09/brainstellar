## Question
32-bit virtual address, 4 KB pages, 4-byte page table entries. Size of a single-level page table?

## Options
- 1 MB
- 4 MB
- 4 KB
- 16 MB

## Answer
**B.** 4 MB

## Solution
4 KB = 2¹² bytes, so 12 offset bits and 20 page-number bits. That's 2²⁰ entries × 4 B = 4 MB.
