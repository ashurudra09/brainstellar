## Question
Which is NOT one of the four necessary conditions for deadlock?

## Options
- Mutual exclusion
- Hold and wait
- Preemption
- Circular wait

## Answer
**C.** Preemption

## Solution
The condition is NO preemption: resources can't be taken away. If the OS can preempt resources, deadlock is broken.
