## Question
A 4-stage pipeline with no stalls runs 100 instructions. Cycles needed?

## Options
- 103
- 400
- 100
- 104

## Answer
**A.** 103

## Solution
k + (n − 1) = 4 + 99. Speedup over a non-pipelined CPU = 400/103 ≈ 3.9.
