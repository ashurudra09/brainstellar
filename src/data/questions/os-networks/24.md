## Question
Correct TCP connection establishment sequence:

## Options
- SYN, ACK, SYN-ACK
- SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK
- ACK, SYN, FIN
- SYN, FIN, ACK

## Answer
**B.** SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK

## Solution
Client sends SYN with its sequence number. Server replies SYN-ACK, acknowledging it and sending its own number. Client ACKs the server's number.
