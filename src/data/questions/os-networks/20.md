## Question
DNS queries primarily use:

## Options
- TCP 53
- UDP 53
- UDP 67
- TCP 80

## Answer
**B.** UDP 53

## Solution
A lookup is one small question and one small answer, so UDP's no-handshake speed wins. TCP 53 is used for large responses and zone transfers between DNS servers. UDP 67 is DHCP and TCP 80 is HTTP.
