## Question
Which CPU scheduling algorithm can cause starvation?

## Options
- FCFS
- Round Robin
- SJF
- (fourth not given)

## Answer
**C.** SJF

## Solution
A stream of short jobs can postpone a long job forever. Priority scheduling can starve too; aging fixes both. FCFS and RR don't starve.
