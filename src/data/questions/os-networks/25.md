## Question
User is logged in but lacks permission for the resource. Correct status code?

## Options
- 400
- 401
- 403
- 404

## Answer
**C.** 403

## Solution
401 'Unauthorized' really means unauthenticated (we don't know who you are). 403 Forbidden means we know who you are, but you aren't allowed.
