## Question
3 processes each need at most 4 units of a resource. Minimum units that guarantee no deadlock?

## Options
- 9
- 10
- 12
- 13

## Answer
**B.** 10

## Solution
Worst case: each holds 3 units and waits for its 4th, using 3 × 3 = 9 units with nobody able to finish. One more unit lets a process finish and release its units. n(k − 1) + 1 = 10.
