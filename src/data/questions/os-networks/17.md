## Question
A counting semaphore starts at 7. Then 20 P (wait) and 15 V (signal) operations run. Final value?

## Options
- 2
- \-2
- 12
- 8

## Answer
**A.** 2

## Solution
Each P subtracts 1 and each V adds 1: 7 − 20 + 15 = 2.
