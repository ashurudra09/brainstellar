## Question
Given known burst times, which algorithm minimises average waiting time?

## Options
- FCFS (first come, first served)
- Round Robin
- SJF / SRTF (shortest job / shortest remaining time first)
- Priority

## Answer
**C.** SJF / SRTF (shortest job / shortest remaining time first)

## Solution
Running shorter jobs first means fewer processes are kept waiting behind long ones. SJF is provably optimal among non-preemptive algorithms, and SRTF (its preemptive version) overall.
