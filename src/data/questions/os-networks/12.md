## Question
Round Robin with a very large time quantum behaves like:

## Options
- SJF
- FCFS
- Priority
- SRTF

## Answer
**B.** FCFS

## Solution
If the quantum is longer than any burst, every process finishes in its first turn and is never preempted, so it is just FCFS (first come, first served).
