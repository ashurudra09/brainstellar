## Question
Random forest mainly reduces \_\_\_\_ compared to a single decision tree.

## Options
- Bias
- Variance
- Training time
- Number of features

## Answer
**B.** Variance

## Solution
It averages many trees trained on different resamples (bagging), which cancels out their individual quirks and reduces variance. Boosting is the ensemble method that targets bias.
