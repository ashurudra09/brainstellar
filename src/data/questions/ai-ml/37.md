## Question
Confusion matrix for a diabetes classifier, n=192: TN=118, FP=12, FN=47, TP=15. What is the false positive rate?

## Options
- \0.0923
- \0.0045
- \0.0872
- \0.1200

## Answer
**A.** \0.0923

## Solution
FPR = FP/(FP + TN) = 12/130.
