## Question
Which is true about k-means?

## Options
- Finds the global optimum always
- k must be chosen beforehand
- It is supervised
- Insensitive to outliers

## Answer
**B.** k must be chosen beforehand

## Solution
You choose k up front, for example with the elbow method. It's unsupervised, reaches only a local optimum that depends on the starting centroids, and outliers pull centroids toward them.
