## Question
A feature user\_location has over 10,000 distinct values. Which encoding is most suitable?

## Options
- Label encoding
- One-hot encoding
- StandardScaler
- Target/mean encoding

## Answer
**D.** Target/mean encoding

## Solution
One-hot would create 10,000 sparse columns, and label encoding imposes a fake order. Target encoding (with smoothing or CV to avoid leakage) gives one column.
