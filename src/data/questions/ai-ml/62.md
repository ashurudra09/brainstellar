## Question
Derivative of the sigmoid σ(x)?

## Options
- σ(1−σ)
- σ^2
- 1−σ^2
- e^−x/(1+e^−x)

## Answer
**A.** σ(1−σ)

## Solution
It peaks at 0.25 when x = 0, which is why stacked sigmoids lead to vanishing gradients.
