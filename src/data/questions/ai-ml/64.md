## Question
Why do random forests usually beat a single decision tree?

## Options
- Faster training
- Reduce variance by averaging decorrelated trees
- Eliminate bias
- Single trees always overfit

## Answer
**B.** Reduce variance by averaging decorrelated trees

## Solution
Bootstrap samples plus random feature subsets decorrelate the trees, so averaging them cuts variance. Bias stays about the same.
