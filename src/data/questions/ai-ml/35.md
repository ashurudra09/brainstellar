## Question
In reinforcement learning, the Bellman equation is used to do what?

## Options
- Approximate kernel similarity
- Compute backprop gradients
- Compute policy entropy
- Evaluate the value of a policy

## Answer
**D.** Evaluate the value of a policy

## Solution
V(s) = E\[r + γV(s′)\]: the recursive definition of a state's value under a policy.
