## Question
In a tool-calling LLM agent, who actually executes the tool?

## Options
- The surrounding program, which feeds the result back to the model
- The model, inside its forward pass
- The tokenizer
- The GPU driver

## Answer
**A.** The surrounding program, which feeds the result back to the model

## Solution
The model only emits a structured call. The harness runs it and appends the result, looping until the model gives a final answer.
