## Question
TP = 40, FP = 10, FN = 20. Precision and recall?

## Options
- \0.8 and 0.67
- \0.67 and 0.8
- \0.8 and 0.8
- \0.4 and 0.2

## Answer
**A.** \0.8 and 0.67

## Solution
Precision = TP/(TP+FP) = 40/50 = 0.8 (how many flagged items were right). Recall = TP/(TP+FN) = 40/60 ≈ 0.67 (how many real positives were caught).
