## Question
For which x is the matrix \[\[1, x−1\],\[3, 2x+3\]\] non-invertible?

## Options
- 2
- 6
- 8
- 9

## Answer
**B.** 6

## Solution
det = (2x + 3) − 3(x − 1) = 6 − x = 0.
