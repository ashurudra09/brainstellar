## Question
Find dy/dx of y = x^x at x = 2.

## Options
- 4(1+log2)
- 4(2+log2)
- 4
- 8

## Answer
**A.** 4(1+log2)

## Solution
d/dx xˣ = xˣ(ln x + 1); at x = 2 that is 4(ln 2 + 1).
