## Question
One-vs-One vs One-vs-All multiclass strategies.

## Answer
OvO trains k(k−1)/2 pairwise classifiers; OvA trains k classifiers (one class vs the rest)

## Solution
OvO: more models, each on less data. OvA: fewer models, but each sees imbalanced classes.
