## Question
Which PCA statements are correct: (i) standardise first, (ii) keep high-variance components, (iii) keep low-variance components, (iv) useful for visualisation?

## Options
- (i),(ii),(iv)
- (ii),(iv)
- (iii),(iv)
- (i),(iii)

## Answer
**A.** (i),(ii),(iv)

## Solution
Standardise first (PCA is scale-sensitive), keep the high-variance components, and PCA is useful for 2-D/3-D plots.
