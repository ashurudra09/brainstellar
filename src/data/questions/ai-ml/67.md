## Question
Why is QKᵀ divided by √d\_k in attention?

## Options
- To keep the dot products' variance near 1 so softmax doesn't saturate
- To normalise the outputs to sum to 1
- To reduce memory
- To add positional information

## Answer
**A.** To keep the dot products' variance near 1 so softmax doesn't saturate

## Solution
With unit-variance entries, q·k has variance d\_k. Large logits give a near-one-hot softmax with tiny gradients.
