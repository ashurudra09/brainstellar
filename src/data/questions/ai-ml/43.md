## Question
Linear neuron with weights 1, 4, 3, inputs 4, 8, 5 and a constant factor of 3. What is its output?

## Options
- 51
- 153
- 54
- 160

## Answer
**B.** 153

## Solution
w·x = 4 + 32 + 15 = 51, times 3.
