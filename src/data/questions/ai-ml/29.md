## Question
Most appropriate cost function for binary classification?

## Options
- Binary cross-entropy
- Hinge loss
- Categorical cross-entropy
- MSE

## Answer
**A.** Binary cross-entropy

## Solution
It is the negative log-likelihood of a Bernoulli output. Hinge loss is for SVMs; MSE with a sigmoid gives poor gradients.
