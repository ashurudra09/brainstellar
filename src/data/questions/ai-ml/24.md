## Question
Which cross-validation scheme fits a stock-price time-series model?

## Options
- K-Fold
- Stratified K-Fold
- Leave-One-Out
- TimeSeriesSplit

## Answer
**D.** TimeSeriesSplit

## Solution
Training folds must come before validation folds in time. Shuffled k-fold leaks the future into training.
