## Question
PCA (principal component analysis) is best described as:

## Options
- Supervised feature selection
- Unsupervised projection onto directions of maximum variance
- A clustering method
- A regularisation technique

## Answer
**B.** Unsupervised projection onto directions of maximum variance

## Solution
It finds new axes (eigenvectors of the covariance matrix) along which the data varies most, ignoring labels, and keeps the top few. Standardise first, or large-unit features dominate.
