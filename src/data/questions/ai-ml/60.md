## Question
What characterises the optimal SVM hyperplane?

## Options
- Minimises margin
- Maximises margin to nearest points
- Passes through centroids
- Minimises training error only

## Answer
**B.** Maximises margin to nearest points

## Solution
A larger margin gives better generalisation, and only the support vectors define the hyperplane.
