## Question
Training loss oscillates wildly and then blows up. Most likely cause?

## Options
- Learning rate too high
- Learning rate too low
- Too much data
- Too few epochs

## Answer
**A.** Learning rate too high

## Solution
Each gradient step is too big and jumps past the minimum to the other side, further up each time. Lower the learning rate or use a schedule.
