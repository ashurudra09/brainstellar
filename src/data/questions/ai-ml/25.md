## Question
Which is a valid assumption of PCA?

## Options
- Components maximise explained variance
- Variance captures the important structure
- Components are linearly uncorrelated
- Data is Gaussian

## Answer
**B.** Variance captures the important structure

## Solution
PCA assumes high-variance directions carry the signal, and that structure is linear. Uncorrelated components and maximised variance are results of PCA, not assumptions; PCA does not need Gaussian data.
