## Question
A is 2×3 and B is 3×2. Which is true?

## Options
- Only AB is defined
- Only BA is defined
- Both are defined, with different sizes
- Both are defined, and AB = BA

## Answer
**C.** Both are defined, with different sizes

## Solution
AB is 2×2 and BA is 3×3. Both exist, but they can't be equal because their sizes differ.
