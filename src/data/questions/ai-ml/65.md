## Question
5% of a numeric feature is missing at random. Best handling?

## Options
- Delete rows
- Mean/median imputation
- Fill with 0
- Drop feature

## Answer
**B.** Mean/median imputation

## Solution
With little data missing at random, imputation keeps the rows without much bias. Use the median for skewed data.
