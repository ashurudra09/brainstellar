## Question
Entropy of a node with 4 positive and 4 negative samples?

## Options
- 0
- \0.5
- 1
- 2

## Answer
**C.** 1

## Solution
−(½ log₂ ½ + ½ log₂ ½) = 1 bit, the maximum impurity for two classes.
