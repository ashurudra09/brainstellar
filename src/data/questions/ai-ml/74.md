## Question
Fraud dataset with 1% fraud. Which metric is LEAST useful on its own?

## Options
- Recall
- F1 score
- Accuracy
- PR-AUC

## Answer
**C.** Accuracy

## Solution
A model that always says 'not fraud' scores 99% accuracy while catching zero fraud. Recall, F1 and PR-AUC (area under the precision-recall curve) all expose that.
