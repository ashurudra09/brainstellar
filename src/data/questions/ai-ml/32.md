## Question
Find the inverse of A = \[\[1,2\],\[3,4\]\].

## Options
- \[\[−2,1\],\[1.5,−0.5\]\]
- \[\[4,−2\],\[−3,1\]\]
- \[\[−1,2\],\[3,−4\]\]
- \[\[−4,2\],\[3,−1\]\]

## Answer
**A.** \[\[−2,1\],\[1.5,−0.5\]\]

## Solution
det = −2; the inverse is (1/det)·\[\[4, −2\], \[−3, 1\]\].
