## Question
Which factors should drive the choice between PCA and t-SNE for dimensionality reduction?

## Answer
PCA: linear, global, fast, reusable on new data. t-SNE: non-linear, local structure, slow, visualisation only.

## Solution
Also: t-SNE distances between clusters are not meaningful, and its results depend on perplexity.
