## Question
rank(A)=2 and rank(AB)=3. What can be said about rank(B)?

## Options
- rank(B)=3
- rank(B)≥3
- rank(B)≤3
- Data insufficient

## Answer
**D.** Data insufficient

## Solution
rank(AB) ≤ min(rank A, rank B), so rank(A) = 2 can't give rank(AB) = 3.
