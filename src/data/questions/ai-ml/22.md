## Question
Find the minimum point of f(x,y) = x^2 + y^2 − 4x − 6y.

## Options
- (2,3)
- (−2,−3)
- (4,6)
- (0,0)

## Answer
**A.** (2,3)

## Solution
Complete the squares: (x−2)² + (y−3)² − 13, so the minimum is at (2, 3).
