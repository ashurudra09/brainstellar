## Question
Linear regression with very few examples but 200,000 features: use gradient descent or the normal equation, and why?

## Options
- GD, inverting X^T X is too slow
- GD, it always reaches optimum
- Normal equation, direct solution
- Normal equation, GD may miss optimum

## Answer
**A.** GD, inverting X^T X is too slow

## Solution
XᵀX is 200,000 × 200,000, and inversion costs O(d³). With so few examples it is also singular.
