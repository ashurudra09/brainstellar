## Question
Xavier vs He weight initialisation.

## Answer
Xavier/Glorot: Var = 2/(fan\_in + fan\_out), for tanh/sigmoid. He: Var = 2/fan\_in, for ReLU.

## Solution
Both keep activation variance stable across layers; ReLU zeroes half its inputs, hence the factor of 2.
