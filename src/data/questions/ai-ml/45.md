## Question
For which a does x+y+z=1, ax−ay+3z=5, 5x−3y+az=6 have infinitely many solutions?

## Options
- −3
- 3
- −4
- 4

## Answer
**D.** 4

## Solution
det = −2a² + 2a + 24 = 0 gives a = 4 or −3. At a = 4 every Cramer numerator is also 0, so there are infinitely many solutions; at a = −3 the system is inconsistent.
