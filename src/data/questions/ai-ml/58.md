## Question
Determinant of \[\[1,2,3\],\[0,1,4\],\[5,6,0\]\]?

## Options
- 1
- −7
- 7
- −1

## Answer
**A.** 1

## Solution
Expand along row 1: 1(0 − 24) − 2(0 − 20) + 3(0 − 5) = −24 + 40 − 15.
