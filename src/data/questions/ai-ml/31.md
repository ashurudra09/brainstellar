## Question
A deployed sentiment model's performance degraded over the past month. Best next step?

## Options
- Switch to a bigger model
- Retrain immediately
- Collect recent data and evaluate model drift
- Increase learning rate

## Answer
**C.** Collect recent data and evaluate model drift

## Solution
Diagnose before acting: check whether the input or label distribution has shifted, then retrain on the new data.
