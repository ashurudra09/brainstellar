## Question
Matrix A has characteristic equation t^2 − t − 1 = 0. What is A^{-1}?

## Options
- Does not exist
- Exists but undetermined
- A − I
- A + I

## Answer
**C.** A − I

## Solution
By Cayley–Hamilton, A² − A − I = 0, so A(A − I) = I.
