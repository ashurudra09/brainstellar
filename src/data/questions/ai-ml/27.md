## Question
How does gradient boosting differ from bagging?

## Options
- Boosting can't use decision trees
- Boosting builds independently, bagging sequentially
- Bagging always more accurate
- Boosting reduces bias, bagging reduces variance

## Answer
**D.** Boosting reduces bias, bagging reduces variance

## Solution
Boosting fits learners sequentially to the previous errors; bagging averages independently trained learners.
