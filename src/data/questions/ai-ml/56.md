## Question
What does AUC = 0.5 indicate?

## Options
- Perfect model
- No better than random
- Always predicts majority
- 50% accuracy

## Answer
**B.** No better than random

## Solution
AUC is the probability that a random positive is ranked above a random negative; 0.5 means coin-flip ranking.
