## Question
Which is a limitation that can significantly hurt K-means?

## Options
- Assumes clusters of similar density
- Handles non-spherical clusters well
- Picks optimal k automatically
- Robust to outliers

## Answer
**A.** Assumes clusters of similar density

## Solution
K-means minimises squared distance to centroids, so it struggles with elongated or unequal-density clusters and with outliers. You also have to choose k yourself.
