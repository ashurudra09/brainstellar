## Question
Does the series sum over n≥1 of n/(n^3+1) converge, and by which test?

## Options
- Diverges by integral test
- Converges absolutely by ratio test
- Converges by limit comparison with 1/n^2
- Diverges by comparison with 1/n

## Answer
**C.** Converges by limit comparison with 1/n^2

## Solution
n/(n³+1) \~ 1/n², and Σ1/n² converges.
