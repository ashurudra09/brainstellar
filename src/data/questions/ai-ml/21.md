## Question
One-hot encoding vs label encoding (listed alongside CSV MCQ round; may be interview).

## Answer
One-hot for nominal categories with few levels; label encoding for ordinal categories or tree models

## Solution
Label encoding on nominal data gives linear models a fake order.
