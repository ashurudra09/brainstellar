## Question
A = \[\[1,1\],\[1,−1\]\]. Eigenvalues of A^19?

## Options
- ±1024
- ±1024√2
- ±4√2
- ±512√2

## Answer
**D.** ±512√2

## Solution
A² = 2I, so A¹⁹ = 2⁹A, and A's eigenvalues are ±√2.
