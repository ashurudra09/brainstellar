## Question
How ResNets (skip connections) address vanishing gradients.

## Answer
The identity skip connection gives gradients a direct path backwards

## Solution
y = F(x) + x, so ∂y/∂x = ∂F/∂x + 1 and the gradient can't vanish entirely.
