## Question
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) mainly addresses:

## Options
- Grounding answers in external or up-to-date documents without retraining
- Faster tokenisation
- Reducing parameter count
- Training from scratch

## Answer
**A.** Grounding answers in external or up-to-date documents without retraining

## Solution
Retrieve relevant chunks (usually by embedding similarity) and put them in the prompt.
