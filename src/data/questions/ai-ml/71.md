## Question
Train accuracy 99%, test accuracy 70%. The model has:

## Options
- High bias
- High variance (overfitting)
- Underfitting
- Perfect fit

## Answer
**B.** High variance (overfitting)

## Solution
A large gap between training and test accuracy means the model memorised the training data (high variance). Fix it with regularisation, more data or a simpler model.
