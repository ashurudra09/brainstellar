## Question
Vanishing gradients are most associated with:

## Options
- ReLU
- Sigmoid / tanh
- Softmax output layer
- Leaky ReLU

## Answer
**B.** Sigmoid / tanh

## Solution
Sigmoid and tanh flatten out for large inputs, so their slopes are close to 0. Backpropagation multiplies these slopes layer by layer, and the gradient reaching the early layers shrinks toward zero.
