## Question
Time to build a binary heap from n unsorted elements (bottom-up heapify):

## Options
- O(log n)
- O(n)
- O(n log n)
- O(n²)

## Answer
**B.** O(n)

## Solution
Half the nodes are leaves and do no work, a quarter sift down at most 1 level, an eighth at most 2, and so on. The total n/4·1 + n/8·2 + … is below n.
