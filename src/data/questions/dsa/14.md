## Question
Evaluate the postfix expression 2 3 4 \* +

## Options
- 14
- 20
- 24
- 9

## Answer
**A.** 14

## Solution
Push 2, 3, 4. \* pops 4 and 3 and pushes 12. + pops 12 and 2 and pushes 14.
