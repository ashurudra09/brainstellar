## Question
Matrices of sizes (2×3), (3×4), (4×1) are multiplied. Minimum number of scalar multiplications?

## Options
- 32
- 18
- 24
- 20

## Answer
**B.** 18

## Solution
(AB)C = 2·3·4 + 2·4·1 = 32. A(BC) = 3·4·1 + 2·3·1 = 18. Multiplying in the order that keeps intermediate matrices small wins.
