## Question
Number of structurally different BSTs with 4 distinct keys:

## Options
- 12
- 14
- 16
- 24

## Answer
**B.** 14

## Solution
Catalan number C₄. With root i, the left subtree has i−1 keys and the right has 4−i: C₀C₃ + C₁C₂ + C₂C₁ + C₃C₀ = 5 + 2 + 2 + 5 = 14.
